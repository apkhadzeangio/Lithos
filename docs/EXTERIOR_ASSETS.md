# Exterior concept assets

These assets were created with the built-in imagegen tool for this project. They are explicitly marked as illustrative AI concepts, never as completed LITHOS projects.

Final served paths:

- `dist/exterior/assets/after.webp`: the hero renovation concept.
- `dist/exterior/assets/before.webp`: the corresponding same-house pre-renovation edit.
- `dist/exterior/assets/modern.webp`, `mediterranean.webp`, `minimalist.webp`, `classic.webp`, `scandinavian.webp`, `stone.webp`: six style previews, extracted from a generated 3-by-2 contact sheet and WebP encoded for delivery.
- The existing `dist/assets/neolith/rapolano.webp` is reused without alteration as a catalog texture; stock, facade suitability and pricing are not asserted.

## Prompt set

Hero: “Wide photorealistic architectural editorial photograph of a premium renovation of an existing two-storey Georgian suburban family house near Tbilisi. Warm ivory travertine and limestone facade, flat shallow roof, slim bronze frames, recessed central entrance, upper-left glass balcony, discreet lighting, planted garden and limestone paving. Eye-level subtle three-quarter front view, whole building visible. Quiet early evening natural light. No people, cars, pools, text or watermark. Illustrative AI renovation concept.”

Before edit: “Edit the exact attached hero photograph into the same house before renovation. Preserve composition, camera, perspective, silhouette, two-floor count, roof edge, all openings, balcony, stairs, vegetation, paving, sky and time of day. Change only exterior finish quality to weathered pale grey-beige cement render with subtle stains, older dark frames and a worn brown entrance door; turn off/remove facade light fixtures. Do not make an abandoned ruin. No geometry changes, text or watermark.”

Style sheet: “One 1536×1024 seamless contact sheet, 3 columns by 2 rows, six equal cells with no gutters or labels. Comparable photorealistic three-quarter front photographs of fully visible two-storey family houses in green gardens: Modern Luxury (travertine/bronze/flat roof), Mediterranean Villa (cream plaster/stone/terracotta), Minimalist (ivory volumes/slim openings), Modern Classic (restrained limestone surrounds), Scandinavian (pale plaster/timber/pitched roof), Natural Stone Villa (honey limestone/dark windows/warm light). Premium quiet natural tones, no people, cars, pools, labels or watermark. Illustrative concepts.”
